    How it works

    1. Collect. Every 5 minutes each server reports CPU, memory, disk delay and temperature.

    2. Predict. Two models look at the past 4–7 days and predict the next 2–24 hours. One uses seasonal smoothing (Holt-Winters). The other learns from recent values (Ridge regression).

    3. Warn early. If a prediction crosses a safe limit, a ticket is opened with a suggested fix. No duplicates while one is open.

    Health score (0–100). It drops as a measure gets close to its limit and drops faster when a breach is predicted soon. Critical means score under 50 or a breach within 6 hours.

    Words used here

    MAE: average size of the miss. RMSE: like MAE but punishes big misses. R²: 1 is perfect, 0 is no better than the average. Shaded band: where the real value should land 95 times out of 100.
